Cliploom

A personal workflow tool that turns long videos into short, vertical, captioned clips, which a person reviews and approves before anything is published.

What it does

  1. Takes in long videos that the operator has the right to use.
  2. Cuts short clips from them, in vertical format, with burned-in captions.
  3. Checks each clip against a set of automated quality and policy rules.
  4. A human approves every clip. Nothing is published without the operator's explicit approval.
  5. Publishes approved clips only to channels and accounts owned by the operator, through the platforms' official APIs (for YouTube, the YouTube Data API).

What it is not

  • It is a tool used by one operator, not a public service. There are no sign-ups, no user accounts and no customers.
  • It does not publish to anyone else's channel, and it does not collect data about viewers or other people.
