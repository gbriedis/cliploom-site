Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

1. Who we are

Cliploom (https://cliploom.online) is a single-operator workflow tool operated by Forest Deer (the "operator"). It turns long videos into short clips, which the operator reviews and approves before publishing to the operator's own channels. Cliploom has no other users and offers no sign-up. Contact: foundersreelhq@gmail.com.

2. What Cliploom accesses

Cliploom connects only to the operator's own Google account and YouTube channels, through OAuth, and requests exactly two scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload: to publish clips the operator has approved to the operator's own YouTube channels.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly: to read the operator's own channel and video information and statistics.

No other scopes are requested.

3. What is stored, and where

  • OAuth tokens (access and refresh tokens) for the operator's own account.
  • Channel and video statistics read from the operator's own channels.
  • The operator's own video clips and their publishing status.

All of this is stored only on systems controlled by the operator. It is not uploaded to any third-party service.

4. Retention and deletion

OAuth tokens are kept only while access is active. When access is revoked, the tokens are deleted. Channel statistics are kept only as long as the operator needs them to run the workflow, and are deleted on request or when access is revoked. To ask for deletion, write to foundersreelhq@gmail.com.

5. Sale, sharing, advertising and AI

  • We do not sell Google user data.
  • We do not share Google user data with third parties.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising.
  • We do not use Google user data to train AI or machine-learning models. OAuth tokens and Google-derived data are never sent to external AI or language-model services.

6. YouTube API Services

Cliploom uses YouTube API Services. By using Cliploom you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Limited Use

Cliploom's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Revoking access

You can revoke Cliploom's access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. On revocation, Cliploom deletes the stored tokens as described above.

9. Other platforms

If the operator connects other platforms, such as TikTok or Instagram, to publish to the operator's own accounts, the same principles apply: access only to the operator's own accounts through official APIs, data stored only on operator-controlled systems, no sale, no sharing, no advertising, no model training, and deletion when access is revoked.

10. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port personal data about you, and to object to processing or complain to your supervisory authority. Cliploom processes no personal data of anyone other than the operator, but you may write to foundersreelhq@gmail.com with any request or question.
